Privacy Policy

SparklePaint is a simple and safe coloring app made for kids and parents. We believe children should be able to play and create without giving away personal information.

What we collect

Nothing personal. We do not collect names, ages, emails, or any personal data.

  • No accounts
  • No tracking
  • No advertising IDs
  • No third‑party ads

Offline play

The app works without internet. Kids can enjoy coloring on a plane, in the car, or anywhere else.

What stays on your device

Drawings and simple settings (like chosen colors or tools) are saved only on your device. We never see or store this information.

Children’s safety

SparklePaint is designed for young children. We keep the experience simple, safe, and private. If you are a parent and have questions, you can always contact us.

Updates

We may update this policy if needed. Any changes will appear on this page.
